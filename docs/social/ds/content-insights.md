# DS content insights

2026-10-08 · @dsmarketing.agency against 24 comparable Instagram accounts · re-run weekly (last section)

## How this was made

- **Data.** 25 files, one per account: ours plus 24 comparables in four groups (local web designers, small-business marketing, Spanish/Latino, SaaS builders). Each file holds the profile (followers, post count, bio) and the 12 most recent posts (caption, media type, like count, timestamp), pulled 2026-10-08. That is 288 comparable posts and 12 of ours.
- **Only likes.** The files have no comments, saves, shares, plays or reach. A Reel's reach to non-followers is therefore not measured here.
- **Hidden likes.** 48 of the 288 comparable posts hide their like count: vilmanunez 12/12, conviertemas 12/12, squarespace 11/12, raiolanetworks 7/12, jobber 5/12 and glossgenius 1/12. Those posts are left out of every number below; their captions still count for format notes.
- **Engagement rate (ER)** = likes ÷ the account's followers today, per post.
- **Lift** = a post's likes ÷ the median likes of that account's 12 posts. A lift of 1.0 is a normal post for that account. ER favours small accounts: 15 of the top 25 come from accounts under 35k followers. Lift compares each post with its own account, so it is the fairer test of what beat an account's usual.
- **Recency.** The ranking uses posts from the last 12 months (2025-10-08 on): 196 posts with visible likes. This leaves out spreadupon's 12 posts from 2019–2021 (the account has not posted since 2021-05-09). Measured against today's followers, those motivational quote cards with 17-hashtag blocks would hold the top 6 spots all-time (2.5–6.1% ER), so the playbook would be learning from 2019 Instagram. kimgarst (last post 2025-04-07) drops out for the same reason.
- **Format is inferred.** The files carry no images or video, so the format column (talking head, screen recording, recap…) is read from the media type and the caption.
- **Classification.** Language was read with word lists and checked by hand. Hashtags and CTA types were counted with patterns.
- **Sample size.** 12 posts per account is small. Treat a difference under about 1.5× as noise; the patterns below repeat across several accounts.
- **Our posts are not in the ranking.** With 144 followers, one like is 0.7% ER, so our posts (best: 25.7%) would fill the top of any ER table. They are compared in *Us vs them*.
- **Quotes** are each post's opening line only (emoji dropped). Nothing from other accounts is reused in our playbook.

1. **Carousels beat their own account most often. Single images do worst**, and single images are what our generator makes today.
   - In 8 of the 14 accounts that post both carousels and Reels, carousels have the higher median ER.
   - That includes all four SaaS accounts that post both. framer: 0.228% vs 0.109%. glossgenius: 0.159% vs 0.041%. toasttab: 0.136% vs 0.114%. jobber: 0.028% vs 0.017%.
2. **People beat product.** The biggest lifts in the whole set are a person's story (44.3×), a personal carousel (9.2×), owner interviews (7.4×, 4.4×) and recaps where people see themselves (4.2×). Feature announcements and webinars sit at the bottom.
3. **Show the transformation or the build.** These all show the result happening, not a description of it:
   - Payton's build demo (2.2×).
   - wix's before → live Reels (2.3×).
   - squarespace's small-shop makeover (its only visible count: 1,353).
   - framer rebuilding a famous site's effect from a prompt (443 likes, 1.2×).
   - framer's "build this in 10 minutes" with no reference point got 13 (0.03×).
4. **Timely and shareable.** Season or date hooks ran 1.9–6.3× (sbagov, glossgenius ×2, toasttab). Slides made for owners to repost were glossgenius's first and third best posts.
5. **Short Reel hooks.** Median caption length: romualdfons 42 characters, toasttab 55, wix 72, paytonclarksmith 107. Ours: 416.
6. **CTAs don't buy likes.**
   - Link in bio is the weakest CTA (0.75). Ending on a question doesn't help (0.97).
   - Keyword lead magnets ("comment / DM KEYWORD") are common: daveyandkrista 9 of 12 posts, vilmanunez 8, conviertemas 8, lyfemarketing 6. Where likes are visible (17 posts), their median lift is 0.97.
   - They are for conversions, not for reach.
7. **Never repeat a pitch within a week** (framer, daveyandkrista, toasttab above).
8. **Posting more doesn't raise likes per post.** Posts a week against the account's median ER:

   | Account | Posts / week | Median ER |
   |---|---|---|
   | jobber | 11 | 0.020% |
   | sbagov | 9.6 | 0.032% |
   | prosperausa | 8.6 | 0.144% |
   | romualdfons | 5.1 | 0.156% |
   | framer | 1.5 | 0.126% |

9. **Hashtags show no consistent effect.**
   - 15 of the 24 comparables use none on a typical post: wix, framer, romualdfons, glossgenius, toasttab, yelpforbusiness and others.
   - Across all 240 posts with likes: 0 tags 1.00, 1–3 tags 0.92, 4–8 tags 1.04. The 9+ bucket (1.33) is 15 posts, 10 of them spreadupon's 2019 quote cards.
