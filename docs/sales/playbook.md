# SDX Inspect — sales playbook (DS Marketing)

Sender: **Joxel Da Silva, DS Marketing**, DSmarketing@wwwdsmarketing.com. Every email and DM is approved by Joxel in the **SDX Sales Pipeline** page (https://claude.ai/artifact/AXaFpXzZgUGiUkVhJhShVf) before it goes out.

## The offer
**Free 30-day pilot** at a few stands / locations. We set up the stands and posters, train the team in 20 minutes, and at the end show them their own numbers: temps logged, problems found, fixed, and with what proof. Then we talk price.

Proof we can say (true, no invented numbers): SDX Inspect runs every day at a 100+ stand stadium in Miami. It was built by the inspector who walks it.
Never name the employer, the venue or its clients. Never use their logos or screenshots with their names. No numbers we have not measured.

## Who and what they care about
| Segment | Who to address | What hurts them | Our one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stadiums & arenas | Director of Food & Beverage / Venue Operations | 100+ stands on event day, one or two inspectors, paper notes, nobody knows if a cooler was fixed | "Every stand checked, every problem fixed with a photo, on one phone." |
| Concession companies | Director of Operations / Food Safety / QA | many venues, many managers, HACCP logs that don't get done | "Your stand teams log their own temps with one scan — and you see who didn't." |
| Restaurant groups | Director of Operations / Food Safety | audits across locations, repeat issues, crews that say "done" with no proof | "Walk it, fix it, prove it — across every location." |
| Events & festivals | Vendor / Operations manager | dozens of temporary vendors, health inspector visits, no time | "Check every vendor booth in minutes, with proof if the health department asks." |

## Hot leads — "talk to me"
A reply is HOT when it mentions price, a demo or call, a pilot date, a contract / procurement / security review, or asks for a decision maker. Then:
1. Stage → `hot` in the pipeline, with the reply text and Claude's suggested next step.
2. Claude stops all automatic follow-ups to that business.
3. Claude tells Joxel right away. Joxel decides and answers personally (Claude can draft).
Out-of-office / "not the right person" → Claude drafts a polite forward request; not hot.
