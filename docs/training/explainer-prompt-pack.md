# SDX Inspect — explanatory video prompt pack ("A game day with SDX Inspect")

Goal: a clean, calm, 2–3 minute explanatory video that shows real people using the system end to end: the inspector on the walk, the concession team scanning the QR poster behind the stand door, a crew member fixing and reporting, the inspector announcing. One look, one pace, one story. Not a hype promo.

## A. Master brief (paste as one prompt)

Make a 2 minute 30 second explanatory video called "A game day with SDX Inspect" for Sodexo Live! at Hard Rock Stadium. Style: clean documentary, bright and natural, like a well-shot training film: steady camera, slow moves only (dolly, gentle handheld), eye-level, shallow depth of field, daylight and warm kitchen light, muted colours, no lens flares, no dramatic music, no fast cuts. Every shot lasts 5 to 8 seconds. People are real stadium staff in dark polo shirts and aprons, shown mid-shot or from behind, hands and phones in focus, no close-ups of faces. The phone screen is always plain (the real app is added later on top), so leave the phone screen blank and well lit. No text inside the footage. Keep the same three characters throughout: the inspector (dark polo, tablet or phone, lanyard), the stand supervisor (apron, cap), and the maintenance tech (work shirt, tool bag). Format 16:9, 1080p, 24 fps.

Structure, in order:
1. Morning. Empty concourse, roll-up gates half open, the inspector walks in with a phone. Text: "Game day starts with a walk."
2. Behind the concession door. The inspector opens a stand's back door, steps into a small kitchen, checks a reach-in cooler, reads the thermometer, taps the phone. Text: "Every stand. Every cooler. On the phone."
3. Finding a problem. A hand sink faucet drips; the inspector photographs it and taps once. Text: "See it, photo it, send it."
4. The board. A maintenance tech in a workshop looks at the phone, nods, picks up a tool bag. Text: "It lands on the maintenance board."
5. The fix. Hands with a wrench under the sink, then a photo of the dry fixture. Text: "In process. Fixed. After photo."
6. The stand team. A supervisor in an apron scans the QR poster taped on the inside of the concession door, then reads a thermometer in a cooler and taps. Text: "Stands scan the poster to log temps."
7. Reporting from the stand. The supervisor notices ice build-up in a freezer, taps the phone, takes a photo. Text: "Any problem? Report it from the same poster."
8. The announcement. The inspector at a counter types on the phone; then, at a stand, a supervisor reads the phone and nods to a coworker. Text: "One message to every stand."
9. Follow-up. The inspector returns to the first stand, checks the sink, taps a green check. Text: "Verified, with proof."
10. Close. Wide shot of the concourse filling with fans, then the SDX Inspect mark. Text: "SDX Inspect. Inspection, done together."

Rules: no faces in close-up, no logos other than ours, no text or numbers in the footage, no product shots of food, no dramatic lighting, consistent wardrobe for the three characters.

## E. Optional narration (calm, plain, one or two sentences per scene)
1. Every game day at Hard Rock Stadium starts with a walk. The inspector opens SDX Inspect and goes stand by stand.
2. Behind each concession door there are coolers and freezers. Each one gets checked, and the temperature goes straight into the phone.
3. When something is wrong, one photo and one tap is all it takes.
4. That report lands on the maintenance board right away, with the photo.
5. The tech taps In process, fixes it, adds the after photo, and marks it fixed with a note.
6. Stand teams have their own way in: the QR poster on the inside of the door. Scan it, log the temps.
7. If they see a problem, they report it from the same poster. It reaches the inspector and the right crew.
8. When the inspector needs everyone to know something, one announcement reaches every stand.
9. Later, the inspector checks the fix and verifies it. Before and after, on the record.
10. SDX Inspect. Inspection, done together.
