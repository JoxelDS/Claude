# SDX Inspect — sales video scripts + shot list (EN / ES)

Ready to record. One 90-second pitch, five feature videos of 30–45 s, and 15-second cuts for social. Every shot says what is on screen, what you say in English, and what you say in Spanish. The Spanish is written to be spoken, not translated word for word.

---

## 0. Read me first

**How to record**
- Screen: record the live app on your phone (iPhone: Control Center → Screen Recording; Android: Quick Settings → Screen record). Portrait 1080×1920 for Instagram / TikTok / WhatsApp; landscape 1920×1080 for a laptop demo or YouTube. Record both when you can — the shot list is the same.
- Voice: record the lines separately with the phone close to your mouth in a quiet room, then lay the voice over the screen recording in CapCut, iMovie or Descript. Speak slowly. ~2.5 words per second.
- Face: an opening and closing shot of you talking to the camera (at the stadium, in a stand) sells more than any screen. Keep it to the hook and the close.
- Captions: always on. Half of the viewers watch with no sound. Use the EN or ES line of each shot as the caption.
- Music: one calm, modern bed under everything, low.

**Assets already in the repo**
- `docs/training/screens/` — 50 real app screenshots, numbered by scene (phone 01–34, iPad 35–42, desktop 43–50). Use them as stills when a live recording is hard to get.
- `public/marketing/video/sdx-explainer.mp4` — the 2:48 silent explainer. Cut clips from it for b-roll.
- `docs/training/explainer-brief.md` §A — the plain-words summary of the system (good for a voice-over rehearsal).

**Brand rule for selling**
Do not use the Sodexo or Hard Rock Stadium logos in a sales video for another venue. Say "built and used every game day at a 65,000-seat stadium". Call the product SDX Inspect. Stand names on screen are fine (they are uppercase in the app; keep them).

**What NOT to show**
- Real phone numbers of stand people (People directory, HACCP tracker rows). Use the seeded demo people below.
- Badge codes on the login screen (blur or cut).
- The update banner ("New version ready") — close and reopen the app twice before recording.

## 4. Recording checklist

**Demo data to have on screen (local mode is fine for recording)**
- Stand: MAGIC CITY DOGS #114 (Concession, license on file) — it is the one in every screenshot.
- Coolers at #114: 2-DOOR COOLER · TRUE · FRONT COUNTER (SDX-CL-114-1), CHEST FREEZER (SDX-FZ-114-1).
- Two demo people at #114: MARIA P (logged today, 10:42) and LUIS R (missed). Phones must be fake: use 305 555 01xx.
- One open plumbing problem at #114 with a BEFORE photo; one fixed problem with BEFORE and AFTER.
- Two more stands with missed logs so "Text all missed (3)" reads well.

**Before you hit record**
- Close and reopen the app twice (no update banner).
- Language: English for the EN take, Español for the ES take. Record the portal twice; the inspector screens can stay English in the ES video with the ES voice-over.
- Phone: Do Not Disturb, full battery, brightness 100 %, notifications off.
- Text size: default. Zoom in post, do not pinch during the recording.
- Every tap: pause half a second before and after so the cut has room.

**Frames**
- Portrait 1080×1920 → Instagram Reels, TikTok, WhatsApp Status, LinkedIn mobile.
- Landscape 1920×1080 → YouTube, email demo, laptop pitch.
- Export both from the same screen recording: the portrait is the phone at 100 %, the landscape puts the phone on the right and the caption on the left.

**Do not show**
- Real phone numbers, real badge numbers, the venue's real license numbers if the buyer is a competitor.
