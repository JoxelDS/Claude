# SDX Inspect — system tour script

Narrated tour of every feature, by role (about 12 min). Voice: Higgsfield seed_audio "Holden". Recorded from the real app with demo data (no venue branding) by the scratchpad recorder train/tour/record.mjs.

## Welcome

### s01 — What this app does

Steps on screen: Inspect every stand · Track every problem until it is fixed · Keep food-safety records in one place

> This is SDX Inspect. It helps you inspect every food stand, track every problem until it is fixed, and keep food-safety records in one place. It works on a phone, a tablet or a computer.

### s02 — How it all fits together

Steps on screen: Inspector saves a report · Problems become follow-ups · Crews fix them with an after photo · Stands log their own temperatures

> Here is how it all fits together. The inspector walks a stand and saves a report. Every problem becomes a follow-up, and it stays on the list until it is fixed. The right crew sees it on their board, fixes it and sends an after photo. And each stand's own team logs its temperatures by scanning the QR poster at the stand.

## Getting in

### s03 — Sign in with your badge

Steps on screen: Type your badge number · Tap Sign In · Crews open their link instead

> To get in, type your badge number and tap Sign In. The app knows your role, so inspectors, crews and admins each see their own screens. Crews don't even need a badge. They open the link the inspector sends them.

### s04 — Menu and language

Steps on screen: Tap the menu button to move around · The bell holds your alerts · The 🌐 globe translates the screen

> The menu button at the top right takes you everywhere: the inspection form, past reports, stands and equipment, messages and settings. The bell next to it holds your alerts. The round globe button at the bottom translates the screen, and stand names stay as they are written.

## Inspection types

### s05 — Pick the type first

Steps on screen: Event walk: quick, temps and food safety first · Regular inspection: full checklist · Post-event: cleaning, temps, food safety

> Every inspection starts with its type. A new one is set to Regular until you change it. On a game day, tap Event walk: it is quick, with temperatures and food safety first. Regular inspection opens the full checklist. After the game, Post-event is a short walk for cleaning, temperatures, food safety and storage.

### s06 — Each type has its own look

Steps on screen: Navy and orange: event walk · Teal: post-event check · Blue: regular inspection

> Each type has its own colour and banner, with a short list of what to focus on, so you always know which inspection you are doing. You can change the type at any time.

## The quick walk

### s07 — Pick the stand

Steps on screen: Scan the stand's QR poster · Or search by name or unit · Type and floor show, license fills in

> Start by picking the stand. Scan its QR poster, tap a recent stand, or search by name or unit number. The stand card shows the type and the floor, the license is filled in for you, and picking the stand counts as being on site.

### s08 — Walk it area by area

Steps on screen: Temps, food safety, coolers, sinks… · Event days put temps and food first · Dots show what is done

> The walk is split into areas, like temperatures, food safety, coolers, equipment, sinks and floors. On an event day, temperatures and food safety come first. The dots at the top show which areas are done.

### s09 — All good, or something's wrong

Steps on screen: All good checks the whole area · Something's wrong: pick the item · Then pick the exact problem

> If an area looks fine, tap All good, and the whole area is checked at once. If you see a problem, tap Something's wrong, pick the item, then the exact problem. Not here skips anything this stand doesn't have.

### s10 — Describe the problem

Steps on screen: Take a photo · Tap what you did about it · Add where, or Urgent on a repair · Tap Done

> A panel opens at the bottom. Take a photo of the problem, tap what you did about it, and add where it is. On a repair that can't wait, tap Urgent. Then tap Done. Flagged it by mistake? Tap It's fine. Tapped All good by mistake? Tap Undo at the bottom.

### s11 — Temperatures

Steps on screen: The stand's coolers are already listed · Type each reading · A bad reading asks what you did

> Open Temperatures, and the stand's own coolers and freezers are already listed. Type each reading, and the app tells you right away if it is good. Coolers: forty degrees or less. Freezers: twenty or less. Hand sink: at least ninety-five. Three-compartment wash water: at least one hundred and ten. If a reading is bad, it asks what you did, like moving the food.

### s12 — Finish and save

Steps on screen: Last time's problems: Fixed or Still there · Tap Finish & save · Every change is already saved

> If the stand had problems last time, they are listed at the top. For each one, tap Fixed or Still there. When you are done, tap Finish and save. You don't have to fill in everything, and every change is saved on your phone as you go, so a dead battery or a lost signal never loses a report.

## The full checklist

### s13 — Every section, in detail

Steps on screen: Coloured buttons are the sections · Each button shows progress · A red number counts the problems

> The full checklist covers everything in detail. The coloured buttons at the top are the sections: temperatures and supplies, facilities, maintenance, equipment, utensils and operations. Each one shows how much is checked, and a red number shows the problems you flagged there.

### s14 — Check or X, row by row

Steps on screen: Tap ✕ on a row · Describe it, pick the location · Corrective action: what you did · Add BEFORE and AFTER photos

> Each item is a list of questions, each with a check and an X. Tap the X on a row and it opens. Describe the problem, pick the location, and write the corrective action: what you did about it. Then add a before photo, plus an after photo once it is fixed.

### s15 — Switch any time, then save

Steps on screen: Bar at the top: quick walk or full checklist · Your answers stay · Before you save: Go fix, or Save anyway

> You can switch between the quick walk and the full checklist at any time from the bar at the top, and your answers stay. When you save, a short list shows any problem still missing a photo or what you did. Tap Go fix to jump straight to it, or Save anyway.

## Past reports

### s16 — Every report, newest first

Steps on screen: Open Past Reports · Stand logs show as Supervisor Log · Search or filter

> Every saved report is in Past Reports, newest first, with its status, the stand type and its problems. Temperature logs the stands send from their poster are here too, marked Supervisor Log. Search, or filter by stand, type or date.

### s17 — Open a report

Steps on screen: Every issue, with photos · Notes, place and corrective action · Tap Edit to add something

> Tap a report to open it: every issue with its notes, location and corrective action, the temperatures the stand logged that day, and all the photos. Tap Edit to add something you missed.

### s18 — Export and share

Steps on screen: Tap Select, pick reports · Excel, PDF or Word · Choose who it is for · Excel can include pictures

> To share, tap Select, pick the reports, and export to Excel, PDF or Word. The app asks who the file is for: cleaning, maintenance, building, Ecolab, or everything. And in Excel, it can put the pictures right in the file.

## Follow-ups

### s19 — Every open problem

Steps on screen: Stand, problem, how long it is open · Before and after side by side · Filter by crew

> Follow-ups lists every problem that is still open, with the before and after photos side by side. Late ones turn amber. Tap a button at the top to see just one crew's problems: cleaning, repairs, Ecolab or temperature.

### s20 — Fixed, remind, photos

Steps on screen: Fixed closes it, Undo for a few seconds · Remind asks for a recheck · Add a before or after photo

> When something is fixed, tap Fixed. Undo is there for a few seconds. Remind sends a reminder that it needs a recheck, and the photo buttons add a before or an after picture.

### s21 — More options

Steps on screen: Group by stand, type, floor or date · Select and export before & after · Put a fixed item back

> More options opens the full view. Group problems by stand, type, floor or date, and filter by crew. See what was fixed today, select items to export a before and after Excel with the pictures, or put a fixed item back on the list.

## Stands & equipment

### s22 — One QR poster per stand

Steps on screen: Posters sorted by unit · Divided by floor · Print a whole floor at once

> On the Posters and licenses tab, every stand has one QR poster. The posters are sorted by unit number and divided by floor, so you can print a whole floor at once. The license list is built in, and any stand without a license is flagged.

### s23 — The people at each stand

Steps on screen: Name, phone and role · Text link: their own stand page · Or the green WhatsApp button

> Each stand keeps its people, with name, phone and role. Tap Text link to send a person their own link to the stand's page by text message, or tap the green button next to it to send it on WhatsApp.

### s24 — Equipment and the verify walk

Steps on screen: Every stand's coolers and freezers · Confirm, fix, remove or add · Not sure: assign in one tap

> The Equipment tab lists each stand's coolers and freezers. Walk it stand by stand to confirm, fix, remove or add units, then mark the stand verified. If the app is not sure which stand a cooler belongs to, it waits in a Not sure list until you assign it with one tap.

## Stand teams

### s25 — Scan the poster

Steps on screen: Scan the QR with the phone camera · Pick a language · Name and phone, then confirm the location

> Now the stand's side. The supervisor scans the QR poster with the phone camera. No app and no password. They pick a language, type their name and phone number once, and confirm the location.

### s26 — Log the temperatures

Steps on screen: Tap a food item or a cooler · Type the temperature, tap Log it · Tap Done: it is sent

> They tap a food item or a cooler, type the temperature and tap Log it. Green means good. Readings reach the inspector as soon as they tap Done, even before they get to the Submit button.

### s27 — Out of range? Send the fix

Steps on screen: A red box asks what you did · Type the corrective action · Tap Send: it turns green

> If a reading is too warm or too cold, a red box asks for the corrective action. They type what they did and tap Send. The box turns green and says it was sent to the inspector.

### s28 — Coolers and supplies

Steps on screen: Fix the cooler list themselves · Ask for supplies · Quantity and Urgent

> If a cooler moved or a new one arrived, they can fix the list themselves. And they can ask for supplies, like test strips, sanitizer or paper towels, with a quantity and an urgent flag.

### s29 — Report a problem

Steps on screen: Type, a few words, a photo · Add another problem · Food Safety Quick Reference

> To report a problem, they pick the type, say in a few words what is wrong, and add a photo and what they did about it. They can add more than one. The Food Safety Quick Reference, with temperatures, storage and labeling, is right there in English and Spanish.

## From the stands

### s30 — What came in today

Steps on screen: One card per stand · Problems and urgent supplies first · Call, or mark supplies delivered

> Back on the inspector's side, tap the From the stands button to see what came in today, one card per stand. Stands with a reading out of range or a problem come first, then urgent supplies. Tap a card to see each reading, call the supervisor, or mark the supplies delivered.

### s31 — Who is not scanning

Steps on screen: Every stand and who logged · Not scanning flags the misses · Text the flagged

> The temperature tracker shows every stand and who sent temperatures today. Tap Not scanning to see the stands that missed their temperatures on event days. Then tap Text the flagged to text each person their own link, one at a time.

## Crews

### s32 — Open the board from a link

Steps on screen: Open the link · Type your name once · Next time it opens by itself

> Crews open the link the inspector sends, type their name once, and their board opens. Next time, it opens by itself. Maintenance, cleaning and Ecolab each see only their own jobs.

### s33 — Working on it, then done

Steps on screen: I'm working on it · Done: add an after photo · Send: the inspector is told

> Each job shows the problem, the stand and the before photo. When you start, tap I'm working on it, so the inspector knows the job has started. When it is finished, tap Done, add an after photo, and tap Send. Cleaning crews also say if they cleaned it or it was already clean. The inspector is told right away.

### s34 — Not mine, and languages

Steps on screen: more…, then Not mine · Pick the right crew · English, Español, Kreyòl

> If a job belongs to another crew, tap more, then Not mine, and pick the right crew. And the board speaks English, Spanish and Haitian Creole.

## Analytics & admin

### s35 — Analytics

Steps on screen: Insights and supplies (Excel) · Predictive · Timeline of everything

> Analytics turns the reports into answers. Insights shows patterns and every supply request, with an Excel download. Predictive shows what is likely to fail next. And the timeline shows every inspection, temperature log and fix.

### s36 — Admin

Steps on screen: Users and roles · Invite links with QR codes · Event days · Announce to the stands

> Admins manage users and roles, invite links with QR codes, and event days. To reach the stands, tap Announce on the Stands page and send a message to every stand, some floors, or the stands you pick. Messages keep the team talking inside the app.

## That's it

### s37 — Inspect · follow up · fix · log

Steps on screen: Inspect · Follow up · Fix with proof · Stands log their temps

> That's the whole system: inspect, follow up, fix with proof, and let every stand log its own temperatures. Inspectors, start your first walk. Crews, open your board. Stand teams, scan your poster.
