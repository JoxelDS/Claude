Privacy Policy

SDX Inspect · Last updated: August 15, 2026

What SDX Inspect is

SDX Inspect is a kitchen inspection and food-safety compliance tool for authorized venue staff. It is operated by JoxelBusiness LLC for use by Sodexo Live! venue teams.

Information we collect

The app stores only the information needed to run inspections:

  • Account information: your name, department, and work badge number (stored as a secure hash) so you can sign in.
  • Inspection data: the reports, checklists, temperatures, notes, and photos you record during inspections.
  • App settings: your venue's preferences, such as theme and inspection schedule.

What we do NOT collect

  • No location tracking
  • No advertising identifiers or third-party ad networks
  • No sale of personal data — ever
  • No analytics beyond what is needed to operate the app

How data is stored

Inspection data syncs to a secured Google Firebase (Firestore) database restricted to your venue's team. Data recorded offline is stored on your device and syncs when you reconnect. Access is limited to authorized staff of your venue and administrators.

Photos

Photos are captured only when you choose to attach them to an inspection item, and are used solely for inspection documentation.

Data retention & deletion

Inspection records are retained for compliance purposes by your venue. To request removal of your account or personal information, contact your venue administrator or email us at the address below.

Children

SDX Inspect is a workplace tool for adults and is not directed at children.
