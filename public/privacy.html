Privacy Policy

SDX Inspect · Last updated: October 23, 2026

What SDX Inspect is

SDX Inspect is a kitchen inspection and food-safety compliance tool for authorized venue staff. It is operated by JoxelBusiness LLC for use by SDX Inspect venue teams.

Information we collect

The app stores only the information needed to run inspections:

  • Account information: your name, department, and work badge number (stored as a one-way hash plus its last four digits, never the full number) so you can sign in. A stand manager may add a phone number so the inspector can text them their stand's link.
  • Inspection data: the reports, checklists, temperatures, notes, and photos you record during inspections.
  • App settings: your venue's preferences, such as theme and inspection schedule.
  • Reports about content: when you report a photo, comment, message or announcement, we keep what was reported, your reason, and the admin's decision, so the venue can review it and tell you what was done.

What we do NOT collect

  • No location tracking
  • No advertising identifiers or third-party ad networks
  • No sale of personal data — ever
  • No analytics beyond what is needed to operate the app

How data is stored

Inspection data syncs to Google Firebase (a Firestore database, and Cloud Storage for photos). The app shows each venue only its own data. Data recorded offline is stored on your device and syncs when you reconnect. Alerts are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple / Google push. The optional language button uses the Google Translate web widget, which sends the text on the screen to Google to translate it.

Photos

Photos are captured only when you choose to attach them to an inspection item, and are used solely for inspection documentation. Photo files are kept in Google Cloud Storage and open from their link.

Data retention & deletion

Delete your account in the app: ☰ menu → My account → Delete my account, then type DELETE. Your sign-in stops working at once, and your name, phone number and assigned stand are removed from your venue's team list. Unsent reports saved on your phone are removed too.

Inspection reports you already filed belong to your venue — they are its food-safety records — so they stay with the venue under the name they were filed with. If you can't sign in, email us (address below) with your venue and your name and we will delete your account within 30 days. See also the Support page.

Children

SDX Inspect is a workplace tool for adults and is not directed at children.
