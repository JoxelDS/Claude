Support

SDX Inspect · Help for inspectors, stand teams, crews and venue admins

Contact us

Email joxelpanita@gmail.com with your venue's name, your name and what happened (a screenshot helps). We answer within two business days.

Try the app without an account

Open the demo venue: joxelds.github.io/Claude/?v=riverside-demo. Sign in with badge 448800, or tap New here? Request access — on the demo your account opens right away. The demo is shared and uses made-up stands and people.

Delete your account

  1. Open the ☰ menu (top right).
  2. Tap My account · reports · delete.
  3. Tap Delete my account, type DELETE and tap Delete forever.

Your sign-in stops working at once and your name, phone number and stand are removed from the venue's team. Reports you already filed stay with your venue — they are its food-safety records. Can't sign in? Email us from the address your venue has for you, with your venue and your name, and we will delete the account within 30 days.

Report a photo, comment or message

Tap ⚑ Report next to it (for a photo, open it full size and tap ⚑ Report this photo), pick what is wrong and send. It is hidden for you at once. Tick Also block to hide everything that person posts on your phone. Your venue's administrator reviews the report, and you get a notification with a summary of what was done — it stays in ☰ → My account → My reports.

For venue administrators

  • Reported content: ☰ → Admin Panel → Reported content. Remove it for everyone, or keep it, and add a note — the person who reported it is told.
  • Remove someone's access: Admin Panel → the team list → Remove.
  • Set up a new venue: ☰ → Set up this venue — the team, the stands, the QR posters and the equipment stickers, step by step.

Common questions

I forgot my badge number. Ask your venue administrator — they can see your account and set a new badge.

New phone? Sign in with the same badge. Crews who joined from a link open the link again.

Language. Tap the language button on any screen. The stand page has English, Español and Kreyòl built in.
