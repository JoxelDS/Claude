How to use SDX Inspect

Stand team: the stand QR page

Every stand has a QR poster. When you scan it, your stand's own page opens in English, Spanish or Kreyòl. On that page you log food and cooler temperatures, fix a red reading, ask for supplies, report problems and log food waste. The inspector gets it right away. Practice on the demo stand BURGER BASE #101. Other visitors share the demo, so use a made-up name and a 555 phone number.

Try it on the live demo

The demo is a made-up venue. Other visitors use it too.

Step by step

  1. Scan the poster and pick your language

    • Scan the QR poster at your stand with the phone camera.
    • Tap the link that opens. You need no app and no password.
    • At the top, tap 🌐 English, Español or Kreyòl.
    EnglishEspañolKreyòl

    The phone remembers your language for next time. Kreyòl is translated automatically.

    Try it in the demo This is what the poster at the demo stand BURGER BASE #101 opens. Nothing here is real.

  2. Say who you are and confirm the stand

    • Type Your Name and Phone Number, then tap Continue →.
    • Check that the stand name and unit on the screen are yours.
    • Tap ✓ This is my location. If you only need to report something, tap ⚠️ Report a problem only.
    Your NamePhone NumberContinue →✓ This is my location⚠️ Report a problem only

    Under Continue → you can see if today's temp log is already in.

  3. Log food temperatures

    • Under FOOD TEMPERATURES, tap a food row (Log it ›).
    • Pick or type the food, type the temperature, then tap ✓ Log it.
    • Each row shows its limit: Hot Holding 135°F or above, Cold Holding 41°F or below.
    • Green means good: the row closes into a green ✓ line by itself, and the reading goes to the inspector (the bottom bar shows ✓ Sent to the inspector). A red reading stays open for its corrective action.
    FOOD TEMPERATURESLog it ›✓ Log it✓ Sent to the inspectorHot HoldingCold Holding

    A bar does not show the cooking or reheating rows. A pantry does not show cooking.

  4. Log your coolers and freezers

    • Scroll down to YOUR COOLERS & FREEZERS.
    • Tap each unit. In the first box (Food item) type what is inside it, for example Chicken. Type the temperature, then tap ✓ Log it. Coolers 40°F or below, freezers 20°F or below.
    • Is a unit wrong? Tap ✎ Edit this unit, then Fix name / location, Moved to another stand or Not here anymore.
    • Is a unit missing? Tap ＋ Add a cooler / freezer that is missing, then ✓ Add it.
    YOUR COOLERS & FREEZERSEdit this unitFix name / locationMoved to another standNot here anymoreAdd a cooler / freezer that is missingAdd it

    The counter on the card (for example 1/2 logged) shows how many units are done.

    Try it in the demo The link the inspector texts when every cooler must be logged. BURGER BASE has a 2-door cooler and a reach-in freezer in the demo.

  5. A red reading: send the corrective action

    • A reading that is out of range turns red.
    • Tap why it happened (for example Door left open), then write what you did.
    • Tap ✓ Send corrective action. The box turns green: Corrective action sent to the inspector.
    • Typed it wrong? Tap ✎ Change.
    🔧 WHY? + CORRECTIVE ACTION *🔧 CORRECTIVE ACTION TAKEN *Door left openSend corrective actionCorrective action sent to the inspector

    The final Submit waits until every red reading has its corrective action.

  6. Ask for supplies

    • In 📦 Request supplies (optional), tap what you need, for example Paper towels.
    • Use − and ＋ to set how many. Type anything else in the box.
    • Tick Urgent — we are out if you have none left.
    📦 Request supplies (optional)Paper towelsTest stripsSomething else? Type it hereUrgent — we are out

    You can send supplies with no temperatures. The inspector still gets the list.

  7. Log food waste at the end of the shift

    • At the end of the shift, find 🗑 Food waste — end of the shift, right under 📦 Request supplies.
    • Nothing thrown away? Tap ✓ No waste today.
    • Something thrown away? Tap ＋ Log food waste. Fill in what it is, How much? (pick the unit) and Why?
    • Tap ＋ Add another item for each other food.
    🗑 Food waste — end of the shiftLog food wasteHow much?Why?Add another itemNo waste today

    An item filled in halfway stops Submit and shows what is missing.

    Try it in the demo After your name and ✓ This is my location, this link jumps to the food waste part.

  8. Report a problem

    • Under Report a Problem, tap the type of problem (What kind of problem?).
    • Say what is wrong and where. A few words is enough.
    • Tap 📷 Add photos to report and fill in What did you do about it? You need both to send it.
    • More than one problem? Tap ＋ Add another problem.
    Report a Problem (optional)What kind of problem?📷 Add photos to reportWhat did you do about it?Add another problem🔴 Urgent

    Tap 🔴 Urgent when it cannot wait.

    Try it in the demo After your name, tap ⚠️ Report a problem only to see the problem form by itself.

  9. Submit, then log again every 2 hours

    • Tap Submit Temperature Log at the bottom.
    • Wait for ✅ Submitted! and ✓ Saved — the inspector has it.
    • Every 2 hours, scan the poster again (or tap Submit Another Log) and log new readings.
    Submit Temperature Log✅ Submitted!✓ Saved — the inspector has itSubmit Another Log

    No signal? It says it is saved on this phone and sends when the connection is back.

  10. Equipment sticker: report a problem with that unit

    • Scan the QR sticker on the unit (cooler, fryer, sink…).
    • Check the unit name and the stand on the page.
    • Tap ⚠ Report a problem with this unit. The stand page opens; after your name and the stand check, that unit is already picked.
    • On a cooler or freezer you can also type Your Name and the temperature, then tap ✓ Save Reading.
    Report a problem with this unitSave ReadingDoing the stand's daily temperature log? Open it here →This sticker is not set up yet

    If it says This sticker is not set up yet, tell the inspector.

Quick check

Three questions. Get 2 of 3 right to finish this guide.

1. You log a cooler at 44°F and it turns red. What do you do?

Coolers must be 40°F or below. A red reading needs a reason and what you did, sent with Send corrective action. Submit waits until it is there.

2. How often should your stand log temperatures during service?

The stand page says it: check food and equipment temps EVERY 2 HOURS. Scan again or tap Submit Another Log.

3. The sink drain is leaking. What does the stand page need before it sends your problem report?

A problem needs its type, a few words, a photo and what you did. If something is missing, the page tells you what to add.

Answers
  1. 1. Tap why it happened, write what you did, then tap Send corrective action. — Coolers must be 40°F or below. A red reading needs a reason and what you did, sent with Send corrective action. Submit waits until it is there.
  2. 2. Every 2 hours — The stand page says it: check food and equipment temps EVERY 2 HOURS. Scan again or tap Submit Another Log.
  3. 3. The type, a few words on what and where, a photo, and what you did about it — A problem needs its type, a few words, a photo and what you did. If something is missing, the page tells you what to add.
