How to use SDX Inspect

Inspector: one inspection from start to finish

Practice on the demo venue, Riverside Arena (demo). Its stands and people are made up. You will sign in, pick the inspection type, answer the guide, log temperatures and save. Then you will follow up on problems, check the stands' logs and export the proof. The inspector screens are in English.

Try it on the live demo

The demo is a made-up venue. Other visitors use it too.

Inspector badge: 448800
Open the demo

Step by step

  1. Sign in with your badge

    • Open the demo link.
    • Type 448800 in the Badge # box.
    • Tap Sign In.
    Badge Sign-InBadge #Sign In

    448800 is the demo badge, and it opens only the demo. At work, use your own badge number.

    Try it in the demo Riverside Arena (demo): made-up stands and people. Other visitors use it too.

  2. Pick the inspection type

    • On the Inspection Locked screen, tap one type.
    • Event day: temps, uniforms and operations first.
    • Regular inspection: every section, in order.
    • Post-event: inventory first, then cleaning and facilities.
    Inspection LockedEvent dayRegular inspectionPost-event

    Every type uses the same full guide. The type sets the order and the priorities; Event day adds a quick game-day card, and Post-event hides the food temperatures.

    Try it in the demo Sign in with 448800. The type cards are on the lock screen.

  3. Say you're on site and pick the stand

    • At the stand, tap the blue box “Tap here — I'm on site, Start Timer”.
    • Or tap 🔎 Pick the stand from the list and type the name or unit #.
    • Or open ☰ → Scan stand QR and point at the stand's poster. The form fills itself in. Then tap the blue box to start the timer.
    Inspection LockedStart Timer🔎 Pick the stand from the listScan stand QR📷 Scan stand QR✅ On-site timer running

    Picking the stand from the list counts as being on site and fills in the name, unit, floor, type and license. A scan fills in the form too, but only the list or the blue box starts the timer.

    Try it in the demo No poster at home? Type 101 in the finder to pick BURGER BASE.

  4. Follow the section buttons

    • The numbered buttons at the top are the sections of the guide.
    • Do the ones marked PRIORITY first.
    • Sections marked “if time allows” can wait.
    • Each button counts what you checked. ⚠ means problems were found. ✓ done means the section is finished.
    Inspector guidePRIORITYif time allows✓ doneTemp rules

    Regular inspection has no priorities, so do every section. Answer only what you see: anything you skip is simply not checked.

  5. Answer each row with ✓ or ✕

    • Tap an item that says Tap to fill in to open its rows.
    • Tap ✓ when it is OK and ✕ when it is not.
    • Or just type the problem in the row. It marks ✕ by itself.
    • Tap BEFORE and take a photo of the problem.
    • Pick where it is, then write what was done under CORRECTIVE ACTION TAKEN.
    Tap to fill inType the problem — marks it ✕ISSUE DESCRIPTIONSPECIFIC LOCATIONCORRECTIVE ACTION TAKEN *BEFOREAFTER📷 Add a BEFORE photo — the crew needs to see the problem

    On a supply row, ✕ just adds the product to Supplies Needed. You can type how many.

  6. Log the temperatures

    • In the Temps section, type the Hand Washing Sink and 3-Compartment Wash Sink water temperatures, then tap Submit.
    • In Equipment, type each cooler and freezer reading. Tap − for below zero.
    • In HACCP Food Temperatures, add the time, the food and its temperature, then tap Submit.
    • If a sink or food reading is bad, a CORRECTIVE ACTION TAKEN box opens. Write what was done.
    Temp rulesKey TemperaturesHand Washing Sink3-Compartment Wash SinkSubmitHACCP Food TemperaturesHot HoldingCold Holding

    Coolers 40°F or below, freezers 20°F or below, 3-comp wash water 110°F or more, cold food 41°F or below, hot holding 135°F or more. The app flags a hand sink under 95°F; the Food Code asks for 100°F, so aim for 100°F.

  7. It saves itself, then Finish & Save

    • After your first answer, the report saves itself. Look for ✅ Saved to the cloud at the top (on a tablet or computer: ✅ Report saved to the cloud).
    • If you try to leave before the cloud has the report, a box says “Your report is not saved yet”. Wait: it closes by itself and goes on as soon as the report is saved. Or tap Keep working on the report to stay on it.
    • When you are done, tap Finish & Save.
    • If “Before you save” lists missing photos or actions, tap Go fix → or 💾 Save anyway.
    ✅ Saved to the cloud✅ Report saved to the cloud☁ Answer the first item — it saves itselfYour report is not saved yetKeep working on the reportFinish & Save💾 Before you save — a few things are missing (optional)Go fix →💾 Save anyway

    The report needs the stand name to save; if it is missing, the top bar says so and shows Add it. With no signal, stay on the report: it saves when the signal is back.

  8. Follow up until it's fixed

    • Tap the 🔁 button at the top, or open ☰ → Follow-ups.
    • Tap a stand's line to open its cards (a Status chip opens them too).
    • Tap a Status chip, like ⏰ overdue, to see only those cards. A chip shows only when it has cards.
    • On a card: 🔔 Remind adds it to one reminder. 📷 Photo adds a BEFORE or AFTER photo. 💬 Comment adds a note.
    • Fixed? Tap ✓ Resolve. Wrong tap? Tap ↩ Undo.
    Follow-upsStatus🔔 RemindSend one reminder📷 Photo🖼 Upload AFTER💬 Comment✓ Resolve↩ Undo

    Picked several cards with 🔔 Remind? The bar at the bottom sends them all as one reminder.

    Try it in the demo The demo already has sample problems in Follow-ups.

  9. Check the stands: temps and waste

    • Open ☰ → Past Reports → Analytics → Temps.
    • A stand has not logged today? Tap 📣 Request temps. It pops up on their phone when they open the stand QR.
    • Tap 📨 Text all missed to text each person their own link (✉ Open text, then Next).
    • Tap 🗑 Waste to see what the stands threw away. 📊 Excel downloads it.
    Past ReportsAnalyticsTemps🌡 HACCP — temp log tracker📣 Request temps📨 Text all missed✉ Open text🚩 Not scanning🗑 Waste📊 Excel

    🚩 Not scanning lists the stands that keep missing their temperature logs.

    Try it in the demo The demo phone numbers are made up. You can open a text, but do not send it.

  10. Export to Excel with BEFORE / AFTER photos

    • Open ☰ → Past Reports and tap ☑ Select (on a phone: ⋯ → ☑ Select Reports). Tick the reports.
    • Tap 📊 Excel, then pick who it is for: one crew, or Everything.
    • Keep 📷 Pictures in the file ticked, then tap Export … to Excel.
    • The file opens on Photo Summary. The Before & After tab puts each problem's BEFORE photo next to its AFTER photo.
    Past Reports☑ Select☑ Select Reports📊 ExcelWho is this export for?Everything📷 Pictures in the filePhoto SummaryBefore & After

    From Follow-ups, ☑ Select to export → 📸 Before & After makes the same kind of photo file for the jobs you pick.

Quick check

Three questions. Get 2 of 3 right to finish this guide.

1. It is an Event day inspection. Which sections are marked PRIORITY?

Event day puts Temps ⭐ and Uniforms & operations ⭐ first. The other sections say “if time allows”.

2. A cooler reads 44°F. What does the app do?

Coolers must read 40°F or below. From 41 to 45°F the app says Follow-up needed. Above that, it is out of range.

3. A box says “Your report is not saved yet”. What should you do?

The report saves itself. The box only waits for the cloud. No signal? Stay on the report. It saves when the signal is back.

Answers
  1. 1. Temps, and Uniforms & operations — Event day puts Temps ⭐ and Uniforms & operations ⭐ first. The other sections say “if time allows”.
  2. 2. Flags it, because coolers must be 40°F or below — Coolers must read 40°F or below. From 41 to 45°F the app says Follow-up needed. Above that, it is out of range.
  3. 3. Wait. The box closes by itself and goes on once the cloud has the report (or tap Keep working on the report to stay) — The report saves itself. The box only waits for the cloud. No signal? Stay on the report. It saves when the signal is back.
