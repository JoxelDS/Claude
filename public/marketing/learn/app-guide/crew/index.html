How to use SDX Inspect

Crews: your job board

Your inspector texts you a link. You need no badge, just your name. The link opens your board: your crew's jobs at every stand, each with the problem and, when the inspector took one, a BEFORE photo. Start a job, send an AFTER photo when it is done, and the inspector gets it.

Try it on the live demo

The demo is a made-up venue. Other visitors use it too.

Open the demo

Step by step

  1. Open your link

    • Open the link your inspector texted you.
    • Need Spanish or Kreyòl? Tap Español or Kreyòl at the top first.
    • Type your name in the box.
    • Tap Open my board. You need no badge.
    EnglishEspañolKreyòlYou are joining asYour nameOpen my board

    Next time you open your link on this phone (or the Home Screen icon you saved from it), your board opens by itself. No typing.

    Try it in the demo Demo cleaning crew. For the other crews, use invite=trymaintenance or invite=tryecolab. Other visitors share the demo.

  2. Pick your language

    • On your board, tap English, Español or Kreyòl at the top.
    • The buttons change to your language.
    EnglishEspañolKreyòl

    The problem text the inspector wrote is translated by the page translator. Stand names stay as written.

  3. Read a job card

    • Find your stand: name and # unit, listed by floor.
    • Read what is wrong under the title.
    • A card can list several problems on the same unit (· 3 issues, numbered). One Done closes all of them, so fix them all first.
    • If there is a BEFORE photo, look at it.
    • ⏰ overdue means the job is late.
    things to doBeforeAfter⏰ overdue

    Tap a photo to see it big.

  4. Start a job

    • Tap I'm working on it on the card.
    • Add a note if you want.
    • Tap Send.
    I'm working on itNote (optional)Send🔧 In process

    Send it when you start: your minutes on the job count from that Send.

    Try it in the demo Demo maintenance board. Already joined another demo crew on this phone? Tap ☰ → Sign out of this phone first.

  5. Waiting on something

    • Tap more… on the card.
    • Tap ⏳ Waiting on…
    • Write what you are waiting for (a part, a vendor).
    • Tap Send.
    more…⏳ Waiting on…⏳ Waiting on what?Send

    Send stays grey until you write what you are waiting for.

  6. Done: add the AFTER photo

    • Tap Done — add photo.
    • Tap Take photo (or Upload) for the AFTER picture.
    • Add a note if you want, then tap Send.
    Done — add photo✅ Done — show itAfter photo requiredTake photoUploadSend

    No AFTER photo, no Send: the button stays grey until the photo is in. Cleaning crew: also tap one of the two buttons in the next step.

  7. Cleaning crew: say how you found it

    • In the Done box, tap We cleaned it or It was already clean.
    • Then tap Send.
    We cleaned itIt was already cleanSend

    Writing 'we cleaned it' or 'already clean' in the note picks the button for you.

    Try it in the demo Demo cleaning board. These two buttons show only for the cleaning crew. Already joined another demo crew on this phone? Tap ☰ → Sign out of this phone first.

  8. Not your job? Move it

    • Tap more… on the card.
    • Tap Not mine → move.
    • Under This belongs to:, tap the right crew.
    more…Not mine → moveThis belongs to:Cancel

    Tap Cleaning, Maintenance or Ecolab and the job goes to that crew's board. Pest Control and Other leave the crew boards; the inspector still sees them in Follow-ups.

    Try it in the demo Demo Ecolab board. Already joined another demo crew on this phone? Tap ☰ → Sign out of this phone first.

  9. More options: search and sort

    • Tap ⚙ More options.
    • Search by unit #, stand or problem.
    • Pick one stand, or switch By Stand / By Problem.
    • Tap Simple view to go back.
    More optionsAll standsBy StandBy Problemshow fixedSimple view

    By Problem puts the same problem from every stand together.

  10. Read reports, sign out

    • Tap ☰, then Reports with … issues.
    • Tap a report to read its problems and photos.
    • Tap Back to my list to return.
    • Shared phone? Tap ☰ → Sign out of this phone.
    ReportsBack to my listRefreshSign out of this phone

    After signing out you need the link again to get back in.

Quick check

Three questions. Get 2 of 3 right to finish this guide.

1. How do you get into your crew board the first time?

Crews join from the texted link with just their name, no badge. After that, the same link on that phone opens the board by itself.

2. You tapped Done — add photo, but Send is grey. Why?

Send stays off until an AFTER photo is in (the cleaning crew also picks We cleaned it or It was already clean). The note is optional.

3. The part you need is on order and comes Thursday. What do you do on the card?

Waiting on… tells the inspector why the job is still open. Done is only for jobs that are really fixed, with an AFTER photo.

Answers
  1. 1. Open the link the inspector texted, type your name, tap Open my board — Crews join from the texted link with just their name, no badge. After that, the same link on that phone opens the board by itself.
  2. 2. You still need to add the AFTER photo — Send stays off until an AFTER photo is in (the cleaning crew also picks We cleaned it or It was already clean). The note is optional.
  3. 3. Tap more…, then ⏳ Waiting on…, write 'part on order, Thursday', and tap Send — Waiting on… tells the inspector why the job is still open. Done is only for jobs that are really fixed, with an AFTER photo.
