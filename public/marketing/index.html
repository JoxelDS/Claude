The inspection guide

How a walk-through works in SDX Inspect — five steps, start to finish.

Sign in with your badge.

Tap in your work badge number — your name, role, and venue load instantly.

Confirm you're on site.

Start the inspection timer so floor time is tracked automatically for the report.

Walk the kitchen with the guided checklist.

Facilities, equipment, temperatures, hygiene, operations — mark items, note violations, attach photos as you go.

Save the report.

It saves itself as you go and lands in History when you finish — share it as a PDF, Word or Excel file whenever you need to.

Track what matters.

Follow-ups list every open problem by stand and crew, with reminders and the crews' AFTER photos; Analytics shows temperature logs, the timeline of fixes and the stands that stopped logging.
